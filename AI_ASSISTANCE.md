## My own work

- **Project idea:** a character builder that helps first-time D&D players understand their character's strengths and weaknesses, and exports the character to Owlbear Rodeo.
- **Platform and rules:** choosing Owlbear Rodeo, the Valdur's Heroic Codex extension and the SRD 5.2 rules.
- **Seven Trials design:** one question per character tag, seven random trials, and pressing Enter to skip a question.
- **Lucky-number design:** the 8-digit lucky number and the dice steps (+ d4, × d6, // d8, squaring, removing a digit, keeping the last 8 digits).
- **Beginner features:** requiring every class, species and background choice to be labelled and explained, Enter for a random choice, and level-1 spells for casters.
- **Testing:** running the program, importing the exported characters into Owlbear Rodeo, linking them to tokens, play-testing a goblin fight, and reporting problems to fix.
- **Documentation:** the Owlbear Rodeo import steps and the play-test procedure in the README.
